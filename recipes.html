Spaghetti Squash with Italian Sausage

High-heat roasted, never mushy. Two servings.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium spaghetti squash, halved and seeded
  • ½ lb mild Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • ¼ cup fresh parmesan, grated
  • Fresh basil or parsley to finish

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Brush cut sides with olive oil, season with salt and pepper. Place cut-side DOWN on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  2. Roast 35–40 minutes until fork-tender.
  3. While squash roasts, brown sausage in a skillet over medium-high, breaking into small crumbles. Add garlic and red pepper, stir 60 seconds. Add marinara, reduce heat to low.
  4. Flip squash cut-side up. Let cool 5 minutes. Pull strands with a fork, then press with a paper towel to remove moisture. This is the key step — do not skip it.
  5. Spoon sausage sauce into each squash half. Top with parmesan and herbs. Serve in the shell.
💡 If squash still looks wet after pressing, spread strands on a baking sheet and roast 5 more minutes before saucing.
