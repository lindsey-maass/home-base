Spaghetti Squash with Italian Sausage

High-heat roasted, never mushy. Two servings.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium spaghetti squash, halved and seeded
  • ½ lb mild Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • ¼ cup fresh parmesan, grated
  • Fresh basil or parsley to finish

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Brush cut sides with olive oil, season with salt and pepper. Place cut-side DOWN on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  2. Roast 35–40 minutes until fork-tender.
  3. While squash roasts, brown sausage in a skillet over medium-high, breaking into small crumbles. Add garlic and red pepper, stir 60 seconds. Add marinara, reduce heat to low.
  4. Flip squash cut-side up. Let cool 5 minutes. Pull strands with a fork, then press with a paper towel to remove moisture. This is the key step — do not skip it.
  5. Spoon sausage sauce into each squash half. Top with parmesan and herbs. Serve in the shell.
💡 If squash still looks wet after pressing, spread strands on a baking sheet and roast 5 more minutes before saucing.
Baking

Brown Butter Banana Bread

Nutty, rich, and never dry. Frozen bananas are the secret.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups frozen ripe bananas, thawed and mashed (about 3 large)
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 large eggs (or 1 egg + 1 tbsp mayo)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
  • 1¾ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp cinnamon

Steps

  1. Thaw bananas completely. Do not drain the liquid — mash it all together.
  2. Brown butter in a saucepan over medium heat until golden and nutty, about 5 minutes. Cool slightly.
  3. Whisk in brown sugar, then eggs one at a time. Stir in vanilla, sour cream, and bananas.
  4. Fold in flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon until just combined. Do not overmix.
  5. Pour into a greased 9x5 loaf pan. Bake at 350°F for 55–65 minutes. Tent with foil at 40 minutes if browning fast.
  6. Cool in pan 10 minutes, then on a rack at least 20 minutes before slicing.
💡 Don't skip the brown butter step — it's what makes this different from regular banana bread. And don't drain the banana liquid when you mash them.
